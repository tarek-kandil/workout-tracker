Appears automatically after tapping "Done Set". Swipe down or tap outside to skip — RPE is always optional.

ℹ Coaching Notes Icon

Small icon in the exercise card header. Taps open a bottom sheet with the coach's notes for that exercise. Dismissed with swipe or tap outside.

Target RPE Badge

Always visible next to the "Set N / N" label during an active set. Shows the coach-set target RPE so you know what effort to aim for.

Post-Set RPE Logging

A small sheet pops up after every "Done Set" tap. Dot-grid selector from 6 to 10 in 0.5 steps. Completely optional — swipe or tap outside to dismiss without logging.

Shown only when relevant

The ℹ icon only appears if the exercise has coaching notes. The "Target RPE" badge only appears if a target RPE was set for that exercise.
