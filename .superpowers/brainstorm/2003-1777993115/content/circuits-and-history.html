Full history — 3 options

How to view all sets from last session without cluttering the card.

Recommended

A — Tap chip to expand inline

A small "Last: 80×9 ▾" chip sits under the exercise name. Tap it to expand a mini table of all sets directly on the card. Tap again to collapse. No navigation required.
